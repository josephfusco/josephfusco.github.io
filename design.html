Rhythm

One unit governs the page, 1.75rem, 28 pixels. Everything vertical is a multiple of it, halves allowed, and borders subtract their own width from adjacent padding. Toggle ⌗ blueprint to see it measured.

Components

  • Live cursor presence

    A human, now, abstractly. It banks into turns, leaves a wake of evaporating ink, and teleports rather than streak across your view.

  • The wire presence

    The flock is the census, one bird per reader, seeded by the page. The species are the wire birds of the reader's own region, told from the clock's timezone, which never leaves the machine. Doves sit still, sparrows hop, wagtails pump their tails, and a pigeon flexes the wire more than a finch. Come close and a bird startles; leave and they all scatter. They come back. The field note under the masthead holds the field guide and picks the region.

  • just you here

    Occupancy chip presence

    Who is here, counted honestly. Your own tabs count as you, never as strangers.

  • Wordmark identity

    Eleven squares wave on hover, staggered by sibling-index(). The square that jumps the queue is a kept bug from the original CodePen.

  • CHEESE!

    Camera Rochester

    The shutter depresses, the flash fires. Press and hold. Kodak country.

  • Favicon chrome

    Each tab wears its own color. The favicon dims when you step away and flashes when someone new arrives.

Patterns

  • Three voices with fixed roles. Bold sans names a thing, serif italic is metadata about it, serif roman describes it.
  • The organizing principle leads. Names first for projects, dates first for archives.
  • The wire under the masthead is the only rule on the page. Everything else is separated by rhythm alone.

Rules

  • At rest, one thing moves at most.
  • Motion is discovered, not broadcast. Hover the wordmark, press the camera, toggle the x-ray.
  • Accessibility is the default, never an option behind a toggle.
  • Everything decorative is recycled from my CodePens. No ornament without provenance.
  • State never changes font weight. Weight is hierarchy; state is color.
  • Reduced motion turns all of it off.
